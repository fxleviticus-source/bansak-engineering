Who We Are

A Zambian engineering company built on precision

Bansak Engineering Enterprises Limited is a Zambian-owned company involved in the manufacturing of bronze billets, bushes, babbitting and other patterns and products requested by customers. The company also deals with fabrication, supply of equipment, engineering, and construction activities — including civil and structural engineering.
