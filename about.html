About Bansak Engineering

A Zambian-owned engineering company established in 2015, working across manufacturing, fabrication, engineering and construction.

Company Introduction

Established 2015. Zambian-owned.

Bansak Engineering Enterprises Limited is a Zambian-owned company involved in the manufacturing of bronze billets, bushes, babbitting and other patterns and products requested by customers. The company was established in 2015 and is managed by Job Sakala.

Beyond manufacturing, the company also deals with fabrication, supply of equipment, engineering, and construction activities — including civil and structural engineering.
