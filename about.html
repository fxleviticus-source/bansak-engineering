Bansak Engineering Enterprises Limited is a Zambian-owned engineering company, established in 2015. The company works across engineering, fabrication, manufacturing, construction and agricultural equipment supply, with a focus on non-ferrous metal solutions.

The business is managed by Job Sakala, who oversees the company's engineering and manufacturing work.
